Operational interpretation:
An accepted EXIT reducing normal count to zero opens the eight-second window.
Zero-count ignored/duplicate EXITs do not restart it. A new post-EXIT candidate
streak must exceed 2s and be camera-confirmed before the eight-second deadline.
Robot release waits for the eight-second check as well as the original five-second
minimum clear delay. During the check, the output uses clearing blink, not steady
occupied red. Existing corrections persist until confirmed clear OR operator count-reset.
The displayed/MQTT occupancy is 1 while corrected. Operator reset clears both
raw counts and the correction, disarms the old window, and retains the usual
five-second release delay. The GREEN button hold timings remain unchanged. Outside the window, late
or missing live detection alone does not create a new occupied indication.
Normal positive counts and original configured fault/maintenance policies still apply.

Limitations: timing filters eligibility; it does not identify a robot or prove a
late detection is not a real person. A real late person is ignored by this fallback,
although normal ENTRY/EXIT counting continues. Camera occlusion and model false
positive ENTRY events are not solved by this timing gate.
